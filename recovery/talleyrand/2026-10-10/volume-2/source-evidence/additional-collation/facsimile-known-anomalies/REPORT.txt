METHOD
pdftoppm -f N -l N -r 200 -png -singlefile. PDF page N = printed Arabic page + 14. Both the Additional Text PDF rendering and the original Image Container PDF rendering were visually inspected for each target. The latter grayscale scan retains clearer punctuation and is the primary evidence linked here. Source scan native detail is approximately 97 ppi; 200 dpi rendering does not invent additional detail.
The transcriptions preserve relevant words and punctuation, while normalizing line wrapping and spacing. Editorial square brackets identify the blank in u745. The u179 superscript marker is transcribed as ¹. Full physical line contexts and original source-unit hashes are in facsimile-known-anomalies/verification.json.
Original digital witnesses are preserved; the comparisons and supplemental readings above do not silently amend them.
