VOLUME II: NOAILLES ARRIVAL AND QU’IL ALLAIT DU SALUT

Both original printed letters are dated November 6, 1814. The ambassadors’ letter states a November1 conference with Noailles arriving that morning; Talleyrand’s letter states his arrival Wednesday morning November2. These differing arrival accounts are inherited from print. No digital date correction or historical reconciliation is supplied by this witness.

Print on p.429 has qu’il allait du salut de la monarchie à¹ ne point laisser, without y. Original note1, corresponding to digital note336, reads Variante : de ne point. No missing y or other corrective main-text wording is supplied.
