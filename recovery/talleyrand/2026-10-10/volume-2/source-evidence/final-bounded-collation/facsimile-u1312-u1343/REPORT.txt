VOLUME II: PRINTED RETURN-TIME PAIR

Both return-time phrases are printed exactly as the digital source reports: avant-hier soir in the ambassadors’ No 9 bis letter and avant-hier à midi in Talleyrand’s No 9 letter. Both printed datelines say 31 octobre 1814. The facsimile confirms a difference between the two published accounts and does not supply a corrective reading or historical reconciliation.
