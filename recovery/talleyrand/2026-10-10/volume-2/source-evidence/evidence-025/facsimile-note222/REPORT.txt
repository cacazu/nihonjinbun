VOLUME II, ORIGINAL NOTE 222: N’EUSSENT / M’EUSSENT

The printed volume and all four digital witnesses agree. There is no recoverable digital typo at this locus. The verified letter difference is distinct from any grammatical explanation of the variant.

Printed main: si elles n’eussent² paru contraster davantage avec sa démarche, son regard et le son de sa voix.
Printed variant: 2. Variante : si elles m’eussent.
The m’ fragment is italicized; eussent follows it. The same emphasis is explicitly represented by the two HTML and two TXT witnesses.

TYPOGRAPHIC EVIDENCE
- Main text contains n’ before eussent.
- The complete printed variant fragment is si elles m’eussent.
- No n’ or ne occurs in the printed variant fragment.
- The note is numbered 2 on this printed page and corresponds to digital note 222.
- All four checked digital witnesses preserve this main/variant difference, including emphasis on m’.

INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY
If the displayed variant fragment replaces the matching main fragment literally, the resulting text changes n’ to m’. Calling it merely an added indirect object while retaining an unwritten negative would require an additional inference. A reconstructed form such as si elles ne m’eussent is not what this footnote prints. The scan does not establish whether the editor intended an ellipsis, a grammatical repair, or any particular change in the force of the sentence; these remain interpretation beyond the printed evidence.
